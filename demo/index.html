Knecht Web Demo

This is an interactive demonstration of Knecht the spider, who lives right inside your browser. He scans the text on the page and physically scurries across the letters with his legs. Try right-clicking anywhere — the spider will head to that spot. And if you double-click with the left mouse button, he’ll start gathering the letters into a line.

This text is also suitable for a stroll. Try hovering your mouse over the spider.
